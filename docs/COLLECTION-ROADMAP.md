# Collection roadmap

## Scope delivered in this change

All 16 Vatican II documents have document-level records and official complete-text links. Eleven have inherited local selections; Christus Dominus, Perfectae Caritatis, Optatam Totius, Gravissimum Educationis and Inter Mirifica are external-only. This completes the bounded source catalogue, not a full local transcription of Vatican II.

Four existing preconciliar selections now have explicit full-source records: Rerum Novarum, Quas Primas, Mediator Dei and Humani Generis. Seven previously absent papal documents have external full-source records: Lumen Fidei, Sacramentum Caritatis, Salvifici Doloris, Vita Consecrata, Rosarium Virginis Mariae, Laudate Deum and Dilexit Nos.

## Next bounded collections

1. Review the identity and provenance of inferred source groups used most often by readers; correct ambiguous titles and dates against primary texts.
2. Establish permission and a reviewed transcription method for the five external-only Vatican II documents. Compare full documents before assigning complete local status.
3. Complete the preconciliar papal source catalogue for Leo XIII, Pius XI and Pius XII, one pontificate and document genre at a time. Define the source bibliography before calling a collection exhaustive.
4. Expand earlier papal and conciliar material, followed by selected episcopal and Eastern Catholic teaching, with jurisdiction and reception recorded.

Private papal scholarship, complete diocesan archives and historical liturgical editions remain longer-term collections. They should not displace review of the existing corpus.

## How priorities change

Readers can save an unsuccessful search or a missing source locally and choose to open a proposal on GitHub. Correction reports, document proposals, and the monthly broken-link report provide explicit evidence for changing priorities. No background analytics or automatic transmission of search queries is introduced. A saved query is not automatically an editorial commitment.
