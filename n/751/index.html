Lateran Council V · 1512–1517

n. 751 Errors of Martin Luther

[Condemned in the Bull "Exsurge Domine," June 15, 1520]

DS 1461Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as heretical, scandalous, false, offensive to pious ears, seductive of simple minds, respectively

11. By no means can you have reassurance of being absolved because of your contrition, but because of the word of Christ: "Whatsoever you shall loose, etc." [Matt. 16:19]. Hence, I say, trust confidently, if you have obtained the absolution of the priest, and firmly believe yourself to have been absolved, and you will truly be absolved, whatever there may be of contrition.

Latin text · DS 1461

146111. Nullo modo confidas absolvi propter tuam contritionem, sed propter verbum Christi: 'Quodcumque solveris' etc. (Mt 16,19). Hinc, inquam, confide, si sacerdotis obtinueris absolutionem, et crede fortiter te absolutum, et absolutus vere eris, quidquid sit de contritione.
