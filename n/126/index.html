Council of Ephesus · 431

n. 126 Condemnation of the Pelagians

DS 267Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Definitive

Can. 1. Whether a metropolitan of the province after revolting against the holy and ecumenical synod.... heeded or will heed the (opinions) of Celestius, this person is in no wise able to accomplish anything against the bishops of the province, since thereafter he is debarred by the synod from all ecclesiastical communion and is rendered inefficacious....

Latin text · DS 267

267Can. 1. Si quispiam metropolita provinciae, derelicta sancta et oecumenica Synodo..., cum Caelestio sensit aut sentiet, ipse nihil amplius poterit agere adversus provinciae episcopos: ut qui iam inde a Synodo totius ecclesiasticae communionis expers sit factus et prorsus inutilis. ...
