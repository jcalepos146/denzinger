Lateran Council IV · 1215

n. 436 Chap. 5. The Dignity of the Patriarchs

DS 811Conciliar decreeEditorial grade · Discipline

Renewing the ancient privilege of the patriarchal sees, with the approval of the sacred universal synod, we sanction that after the Roman Church, which by the ordering of the Lord before all others holds the first place of ordinary power as the mother and teacher of all the faithful of Christ, the (Church of) Constantinople holds the first, Alexandria the second, Antioch the third, and Jerusalem the fourth place.
