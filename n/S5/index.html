Supplement S5 St. Odilo of Cluny at the election of Clement II and the coronation of Henry III

[Iotsald of Saint-Claude, Vita des Abtes Odilo von Cluny II, in MGH SS rer. Germ. 68, p. 235. Translation and citation contributed to this site; not yet checked against the edition.]

Not in DenzingerEditorial grade · Historical witness

And having completed his devout prayer, he [St. Odilo] was present at the election of lord Clement [II] and, acting with the emperor along with the courtly primates, he judged it worthy for the aforesaid apostolic man to ascend the throne. On the morrow, however, when the entire race of Christians celebrates God made man and born of the holy virgin, he came in order to see King Henry being crowned with the imperial diadem. He stood present at his sacred anointing, giving glory to God, who wished to strengthen the Roman empire, with a most just prelate chosen and a Catholic prince of the republic, now that the whirlwinds of evils had been quieted.

Editorial assessment · Historical witness. Odilo of Cluny, the leading reformer of his day, took part in the election of Clement II and saw it as the end of a crisis. Witness to how the reform party received Sutri.
