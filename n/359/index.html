Lateran Council I · 1123

n. 359 Simony, Celibacy, Investiture, Incest

DS 710Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Discipline

Can. 1. "Following the examples of the Holy Fathers" and renewing the duty of our office "we forbid in every way by the authority of the Apostolic See that anyone by means of money be ordained or promoted in the Church of God. But if anyone shall have acquired ordination or promotion in the Church in this way, let him be entirely deprived of his office." *

Latin text · DS 710

710Can. 1. 'Sanctorum Patrum exempla sequentes' et officii nostri debita innovantes, 'ordinari quemquam per pecuniam in Ecclesia Dei vel promoveri, auctoritate Sedis Apostolicae modis omnibus prohibemus. Si quis vero in Ecclesia ordinationem vel promotionem taliter acquisierit, acquisita (-!) prorsus careat dignitate'.
