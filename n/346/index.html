St. Leo IX · 1049–1054

n. 346 Symbol of Faith

[From the epistle "Congratulamur vehementer" to Peter, Bishop of Antioch, April 13, 1053]

DS 683CreedEditorial grade · Profession of faith

That this holy and individual Trinity is not three Gods, but in three persons and in one nature or essence [is] one God omnipotent, external, invisible and incommutable, so I believe and confess, so that I may truly proclaim that the Father is not begotten, the Son is the only begotten one, and the Holy Spirit is neither begotten nor unbegotten, but proceeds from the Father and the Son.

Latin text · DS 683

683Hanc sanctam et individuam Trinitatem non tres Deos, sed in tribus personis et in una natura sive essentia unum Deum omnipotentem, aeternum, invisibilem et incommutabilem ita credo et confiteor, ut Patrem ingenitum, Filium unigenitum, Spiritum Sanctum nec genitum nec ingenitum, sed a Patre et Filio procedentem, veraciter praedicem.
