John Paul II · 1978–2005

VS 45 Jesus Christ, the true light that enlightens everyone

[From the Encyclical Letter, "Veritatis Splendor," Aug. 6, 1993]

Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

The Church gratefully accepts and lovingly preserves the entire deposit of Revelation, treating it with religious respect and fulfilling her mission of authentically interpreting God's law in the light of the Gospel. In addition, the Church receives the gift of the New Law, which is the "fulfilment" of God's law in Jesus Christ and in his Spirit. This is an "interior" law (cf. Jer 31:31-33), "written not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on tablets of stone but on tablets of human hearts" (2 Cor 3:3); a law of perfection and of freedom (cf. 2 Cor 3:17); "the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus" ( Rom 8:2). ...

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (VS 22–VS 120). The foundations of moral theology: freedom and law, conscience, the fundamental option, and intrinsically evil acts which no intention or circumstance can make good (VS 80). Many theologians hold the teaching on intrinsically evil acts to be definitive; the encyclical presents it as the constant teaching of the Church.
