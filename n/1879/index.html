Leo XIII · 1878–1903

n. 1879 The Christian Constitution of States

[From the Encyclical "Immortale Dei," November 1, 1885]

cf. DS 3181Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

In these studies the Church is not in opposition if the mind discovers something new; she does not object to further investigations being made for the refinements and comforts of life; rather, as an enemy of indolence and sloth she wishes especially that the talents of man bear rich fruits by exercise and cultivation; she furnishes incentives to all kinds of arts and works; and by directing through her influence all zeal for such things towards virtue and salvation, she struggles to prevent man from being turned away from God and heavenly blessings by his intelligence and industry....
