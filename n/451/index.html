Council of Lyons I · 1245

n. 451 The Rites of the Greeks

[From the letter "Sub Catholicae" to the Bishop of Tusculum, of the Legation of the Apostolic See among the Greeks, March 6, 1254]

DS 832–833Conciliar decreeEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

6. Moreover no one may merely be anointed with some unction by priests or confessors for satisfaction of penance—7. But upon the sick according to the word of James the Apostle [ Jas. 5:4] let extreme unction be conferred.

Latin text · DS 832–833

8326. Nullus autem per sacerdotes vel confessores pro satisfactione paenitentiae unctione aliqua solummodo inungatur.

8337. Infirmis vero iuxta verbum Iacobi Apostoli (Iac 5, 14 s) unctio exhibeatur extrema.
