John XXIII · 1958–1963

PT 54 Attainment of the Common Good is the Purpose of the Public Authority

[From the Encyclical Letter, "Pacem in Terris," April 11, 1963]

DS 3983–3984Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

The attainment of the common good is the sole reason for the existence of civil authorities. In working for the common good, therefore, the authorities must obviously respect its nature, and at the same time adjust their legislation to meet the requirements of the given situation.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (PT 5–PT 61). The order written in human nature; the rights and duties that flow from personal dignity; authority as coming from God, binding in conscience only within the moral order; the common good as the reason for civil authority. The natural law teaching of the encyclical. Its list of rights is teaching; how each is to be secured is left to circumstances.
