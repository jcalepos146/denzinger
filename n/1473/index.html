Benedict XIV · 1740–1758

n. 1473 Profession of Faith which Is Prescribed for Orientals (Maronites)

[From the Constitution, "Nuper ad nos.,, March 16. 1743]

DS 2540CreedEditorial grade · Profession of faith

Likewise, all other things I accept and profess, which the Holy Roman Church accepts and professes, and I likewise condemn, reject, and anathematize, at the same time all contrary things, both schisms and heresies, which have been condemned, rejected, and anathematized by the same Church. In addition, I promise and swear true obedience to the Roman Pontiff, the successor of Blessed Peter, the prince of the Apostles and the vicar of Jesus Christ. And that this faith of the Catholic Church, without which no one can be saved, etc.... [as in the Tridentine profession of faith, see n. 1000 ].

Latin text · DS 2540

2540Cetera item omnia suscipio et profiteor, quae recipit et profitetur sancta Romana Ecclesia, simulque contraria omnia, et schismata et haereses ab eadem Ecclesia damnatas, reiectas et anathematizatas ego pariter damno, reicio et anathematizo. Insuper Romano Pontifici beati Petri principis Apostolorum successori ac Jesu Christi vicario veram oboedientiam spondeo ac iuro. Hanc fidem catholicae Ecclesiae, extra quam nemo salvus esse potest, etc. (ut in professione fidei Tridentina, DS 1870ss). 1.509
