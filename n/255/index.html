Lateran Synod of 649 · 649

n. 255 The Trinity, the Incarnation, etc.

Particular councilEditorial grade · Definitive

Can. 2. If anyone does not properly and truly confess in accordance with the Holy Fathers that God the Word himself, one of the holy and consubstantial and venerable Trinity, descended from heaven, and was incarnate of the Holy Spirit and Mary ever Virgin, and was made man, was crucified in the flesh, voluntarily suffered for us and was buried, and arose again on the third day, and ascended into heaven, and sits at the right hand of the Father, and will come again with paternal glory, with his flesh assumed by Him and intellectually animated, to judge the living and the dead, let him be condemned [see n. 2, 6, 65,215].

Editorial assessment · Definitive (n. 254–274). The canons of the Lateran Synod of 649 under Martin I against monothelitism. Received by Constantinople III; their doctrine, including Mary's perpetual virginity (n. 256), is held as of faith.
