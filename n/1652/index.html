Pius IX · 1846–1878

n. 1652 False Traditionalism (against Augustine Bonnetty)

[From the Decree of the S.C. of the Index, 11, (15) June, 1855]

DS 2814Papal teachingEditorial grade · Profession of faith

4. The method which St. Thomas and St. Bonaventure and other scholastics after them used does not lead to rationalism, nor has it been the reason why philosophy in today's schools is falling into naturalism and pantheism. Therefore, it is not lawful to charge as a reproach against these doctors and teachers that they made use of this method, especially since the Church approves, or at least keeps silent.*

Latin text · DS 2814

28144. Methodus, qua usi sunt D. Thomas, D. Bonaventura et alii post ipsos scholastici, non ad rationalismum ducit, neque causa fuit, cur apud scholas hodiernas philosophia in naturalismum et pantheismum impingeret. Proinde non licet in crimen doctoribus et magistris illis vertere, quod methodum hanc, praesertim approbante vel saltem tacente Ecclesia, usurpaverint. 1.582 1.583
