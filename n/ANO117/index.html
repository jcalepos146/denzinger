ANO 117 True Wisdom

[From the Note of the Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith and the Dicastery for Culture and Education on the relationship between artificial intelligence and human intelligence, "Antiqua et Nova," Jan. 28, 2025]

Doctrine of the FaithEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

From this perspective of wisdom, believers will be able to act as moral agents capable of using this technology to promote an authentic vision of the human person and society. This should be done with the understanding that technological progress is part of God’s plan for creation—an activity that we are called to order toward the Paschal Mystery of Jesus Christ, in the continual search for the True and the Good.

The Supreme Pontiff, Francis, at the Audience granted on 14 January 2025 to the undersigned Prefects and Secretaries of the Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith and the Dicastery for Culture and Education, approved this Note and ordered its publication.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (ANO 30–ANO 117). Human intelligence is not reducible to computation; AI is not intelligence in the human sense; the use of AI must serve the person and remain under human responsibility.

Source and coverage: Antiqua et Nova · unreviewed locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
