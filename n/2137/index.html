Pius X · 1903–1914

n. 2137 The Age for Admitting to First Eucharistic Communion

[From the Decree, "Quem singular)," of the Congregation on the Sacraments, August 8, 1910]

DS 3530Papal teachingEditorial grade · Discipline

I. The age of discretion both for confession and for Holy Communion is that at which the child begins to reason, that is, at about the seventh year, more or less. The obligation of satisfying both precepts of confession and communion begins from that time [see n. 437].

Latin text · DS 3530

3530I. Aetas discretionis tum ad confessionem tum ad s. communionem ea est, in qua puer incipit ratiocinari, hoc est circa septimum annum, sive supra, sive etiam infra. Ex hoc tempore incipit obligatio satisfaciendi utrique praecepto confessionis et communionis (vd. DS 812).
