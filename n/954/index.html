Pius IV · 1559–1565 · Session XXII (Sept. 17, 1562)

n. 954 Canons on the Most Holy Sacrifice of the Mass

DS 1757Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

Can. 7. If anyone says that the ceremonies, vestments, and outward signs, which the Catholic Church uses in the celebration of Masses, are incentives to impiety rather than the services of piety: let him be anathema [cf. n.943 ].

Latin text · DS 1757
