Pius X · 1903–1914

n. 1989 The Daily Partaking of the Most Holy Eucharist

[From the Decree of the Congregation of the Holy Council, approved by Pius X December 20th, 1905]

DS 3383Curial responseEditorial grade · Discipline

5... Let the counsel of the confessor intercede. Yet let confessors beware lest they turn anyone away from frequent or daily communion, who is found in the state of grace and approaches (it) with a right mind....
