Innocent XI · 1676–1689

n. 1288 Errors of Michael of Molinos

[Condemned in the decree of the Sacred Office, August 28, and in the Constitutions "Coelestis Pastor," Nov. 20, 1687]

DS 2268Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as heretical, erroneous, scandalous, blasphemous, offensive to pious ears, rash, seditious, respectively

68. In the world there is neither faculty nor jurisdiction for commanding that the letters of a director, as far as the interior direction of a soul is concerned, should be made manifest; therefore, it is necessary to assert that it is an insult of Satan, etc.

Condemnedas heretical, suspect, erroneous, scandalous, blasphemous, offensive to pious ears, rash, of relaxed Christian discipline, subversive, and seditious respectively.
