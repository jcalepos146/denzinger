John XXIII · 1958–1963

PT 13 Rights Pertaining to Moral and Cultural Values

[From the Encyclical Letter, "Pacem in Terris," April 11, 1963]

DS 3960Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

He has the natural right to share in the benefits of culture, and hence to receive a good general education, and a technical or professional training consistent with the degree of educational development in his own country. Furthermore, a system must be devised for affording gifted members of society the opportunity of engaging in more advanced studies, with a view to their occupying, as far as possible, positions of responsibility in society in keeping with their natural talent and acquired skill.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (PT 5–PT 61). The order written in human nature; the rights and duties that flow from personal dignity; authority as coming from God, binding in conscience only within the moral order; the common good as the reason for civil authority. The natural law teaching of the encyclical. Its list of rights is teaching; how each is to be secured is left to circumstances.
