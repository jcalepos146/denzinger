The Vatican Council · 1869–1870 · Session III (April 24, 1870)

n. 1816 Chap. 4. Faith and reason

DS 3041Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

1. If anyone shall have said that no true mysteries properly so-called are contained in divine revelation, but that all the dogmas of faith can be understood and proved from natural principles, through reason properly cultivated: let him be anathema [cf. n.1795f.].
