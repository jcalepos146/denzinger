The Second Vatican Council · 1962–1965

GS 39 Man's Activity Throughout the World

[From the Pastoral Constitution on the Church in the Modern World, "Gaudium et Spes," Dec. 7, 1965]

Conciliar decreeEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

We do not know the time for the consummation of the earth and of humanity, nor do we know how all things will be transformed. As deformed by sin, the shape of this world will pass away; but we are taught that God is preparing a new dwelling place and a new earth where justice will abide, and whose blessedness will answer and surpass all the longings for peace which spring up in the human heart. Then, with death overcome, the sons of God will be raised up in Christ, and what was sown in weakness and corruption will be invested with incorruptibility. Enduring with charity and its fruits, all that creation which God made on man's account will be unchained from the bondage of vanity. ...

On this earth that Kingdom is already present in mystery. ...

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (GS 12–GS 69). The human person made in God's image, one in body and soul; conscience; freedom; death; atheism; Christ who reveals man to himself (GS 22); the human community, the autonomy of earthly realities rightly understood, the Church and the world; marriage and conjugal love, with the rejection of abortion and of methods of regulating birth the Magisterium disapproves (GS 51); the universal destination of goods (GS 69).
