Council of Basel and its aftermath · 1431–1463 · 1433–1436

Supplement B4 The Compacts with the Hussites: the chalice for Bohemia

[Summary written for this site.]

Not in DenzingerEditorial grade · Discipline

In agreements made at Prague (1433) and solemnly promulgated at Iglau (5 July 1436), the council allowed the Utraquists of Bohemia and Moravia to receive Communion under both kinds, on condition that they also taught that Christ is received whole and entire under each kind, and settled their other demands on preaching, clerical property and the punishment of public sins.

Pius II declared the Compacts void in 1462.

Editorial assessment · Discipline. A disciplinary concession that kept the doctrine defined at Constance (n. 626) and later at Trent (n. 930–937): Communion under one kind is complete, and the chalice for the laity is a matter of the Church's discipline.
