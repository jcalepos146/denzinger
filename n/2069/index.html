Pius X · 1903–1914

n. 2069 Betrothal and Marriage

[From the Decree Ne temere of the Holy Council, August 2, 1907]

DS 3471Papal teachingEditorial grade · Discipline

Vlll. If it happens that in some region the pastor or ordinary of the place or priest delegated by them, in the presence of whom marriage can be celebrated, cannot be had, and this condition of things has lasted now for a month, the marriage can be validly and licitly entered upon after a formal consent has been given by the betrothed in the presence of two witnesses.
