John XXIII · 1958–1963

PT 98 Active Solidarity

[From the Encyclical Letter, "Pacem in Terris," April 11, 1963]

Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

Since relationships between States must be regulated in accordance with the principles of truth and justice, States must further these relationships by taking positive steps to pool their material and spiritual resources. In many cases this can be achieved by all kinds of mutual collaboration; and this is already happening in our own day in the economic, social, political, educational, health and athletic spheres—and with beneficial results. We must bear in mind that of its very nature civil authority exists, not to confine men within the frontiers of their own nations, but primarily to protect the common good of the State, which certainly cannot be divorced from the common good of the entire human family

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (PT 73–PT 98). Citizens' right to take part in public life, and relations between states governed by truth, justice and active solidarity.

Source and coverage: Pacem in Terris · unreviewed locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
