Leo XIII · 1878–1903

n. 1968 Americanism

[From the Letter, "Testem benevolentiae," to Cardinal Gibbons, January 22, 1899]

DS 3341Papal teachingEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censures

Now the history of all past ages is witness that this Apostolic See, to which not only the office of teaching, but also the supreme government of the whole Church were assigned, has indeed continually adhered "to the same doctrine, in the same sense, and in the same mind" [Cone. Vatic., see n. 1800]; that it has always been accustomed to modify the rule of life so as never to overlook the manners and customs of the various peoples which it embraces, while keeping the divine law unimpaired. If the safety of souls demands this, who will doubt that it will do so now?— This, however, is not to be determined by the decision of private individuals
