Pius XI · 1922–1939

n. 2199 Meetings to Procure the Unity of All Christians

[From the Decree of the Holy Office, July 8, 1927]

Curial responseEditorial grade · Discipline

Whether it is permitted Catholics to be present at, or to take part in conventions, gatherings, meetings, or societies of non-Catholics which aim to associate together under a single agreement all who in any way lay claim to the name of Christian?

Reply:In the negative, and there must be complete adherence to the decree ( De participatione catholicorum societati,"ad procurandam christianitatis unitatem") on the participation of Catholics in a society "to procure the unity of Christianity." *

Editorial assessment · Discipline. The Holy Office forbids Catholics to take part in non-Catholic unity conferences (1927), a discipline later changed.

Source and coverage: Meetings to Procure the Unity of All Christians · unreviewed locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
