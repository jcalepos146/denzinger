The Second Vatican Council · 1962–1965

SC 74 The Other Sacraments and the Sacramentals

[From the Constitution on the Sacred Liturgy, "Sacrosanctum Concilium," Dec. 4, 1963]

Conciliar decreeEditorial grade · Discipline

In addition to the separate rites for anointing of the sick and for viaticum, a continuous rite shall be prepared according to which the sick man is anointed after he has made his confession and before he receives viaticum.

Editorial assessment · Discipline (SC 72–SC 75). Revision of the rites of penance and of the anointing of the sick, which "may also and more fittingly" be so called: liturgical reform.

Source and coverage: Sacrosanctum Concilium · excerpts locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
