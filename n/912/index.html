Julius III · 1550–1555 · Session XIV (Nov. 25, 1551)

n. 912 Canons On the Sacrament of Penance

DS 1702Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

Can. 2. If anyone, confusing the sacraments, says that baptism itself is the sacrament of penance, as though these two sacraments are not distinct, and that therefore penance is not rightly called "a second plank after shipwreck": let him be anathema [cf.n. 894 ].

Latin text · DS 1702
