Council of Basel and its aftermath · 1431–1463 · 18 September and 30 December 1437

Supplement B7 Eugene IV transfers the council to Ferrara

[Summary written for this site.]

Not in DenzingerEditorial grade · Juridical act

By the bull Doctoris gentium (18 September 1437) Eugene IV transferred the council to Ferrara, to meet the Greeks, and confirmed the transfer on 30 December 1437. The minority at Basel, with the papal legates, followed him; the majority stayed and continued to meet.

Editorial assessment · Juridical act. Most Catholic historians treat Basel as without authority from the transfer onward; Lateran V gives this as the reason its later acts have no weight (n. 740).

Source and coverage: The Compacts with the Hussites: the chalice for Bohemia · summary locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
