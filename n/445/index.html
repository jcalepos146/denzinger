Gregory IX · 1227–1241

n. 445 The Matter and Form of Ordination

[From the letter to Olaus, Bishop of Lyons, Dec. 9, 1232]

DS 826Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

When a priest and deacon are ordained, they receive the imposition of a hand by corporal touch, by the rite introduced by the Apostles; and if this shall be omitted, it must not be partially repeated, but at an established time for conferring orders of this kind, what through error was omitted must be carefully supplied. Moreover, the suspension of hands over the head must be made, when the prayer of ordination is uttered over the head.

Latin text · DS 826

826Presbyter et diaconus cum ordinantur, manus impositionem tactu corporali, ritu ab Apostolis introducto (cf. 1 Tim 4,14; 5, 22; 2 Tim 1, 6; Act 6, 6), recipiunt; quod si omissum fuerit, non est aliquatenus iterandum, sed statuto tempore ad huiusmodi ordines conferendos, caute supplendum quod per errorem exstitit praetermissum. Suspensio autem manuum debet fieri, cum oratio super caput effunditur ordinandi. 1.288
