Innocent XI · 1676–1689

n. 1254 Errors of Michael of Molinos

[Condemned in the decree of the Sacred Office, August 28, and in the Constitutions "Coelestis Pastor," Nov. 20, 1687]

DS 2234Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as heretical, erroneous, scandalous, blasphemous, offensive to pious ears, rash, seditious, respectively

34. To give thanks to God by words and by speech is not for interior souls which ought to remain in silence, placing no obstacle before God, because He operates in them; and the more they resign themselves to God, they discover that they cannot recite the Lord's prayer, i.e., the Our Father.

Latin text · DS 2234

223434. Verbis et lingua gratias agere Deo, non est pro animabus internis, quae in silentio manere debent, nullum Deo impedimentum opponendo (apponendo), quod operetur in illis; et quo magis Deo se resignant, experiuntur, se non posse orationem dominicam seu Pater noster recitare.
