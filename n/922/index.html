Julius III · 1550–1555 · Session XIV (Nov. 25, 1551)

n. 922 Canons On the Sacrament of Penance

DS 1712Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

Can. 12. If anyone says that the whole punishment, together with the guilt, is always pardoned by God, and that the satisfaction of penitents is nothing other than faith, by which they perceive that Christ has made satisfaction for them: let him be anathema [cf. n. 904 ].
