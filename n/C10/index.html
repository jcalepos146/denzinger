Supplement C10 Martin V names Alexander V "our predecessor"

[Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Reg. Lat. 187, fol. 1, 1v, a confirmation at the opening of Martin V's register. Translation and citation contributed to this site; not yet checked against the scan.]

Not in DenzingerEditorial grade · Historical witness

Latin: "Alexander papa V predecessor noster … et deinde Baldassar tunc Johannes papa XXIII, qui dicto predecessore ab hac luce sublato in papatu immediate successit … nobis, qui post depositionem dicti Baldassaris a papatu fuimus divina favente clementia ad apicem summi apostolatus assumpti."

Pope Alexander V, our predecessor … and then Baldassarre, at that time Pope John XXIII, who, when the said predecessor was taken from this life, immediately succeeded him in the papacy … we, who after the deposition of the said Baldassarre from the papacy were, by the favor of divine mercy, raised to the summit of the supreme apostolate.

Editorial assessment · Historical witness. Chancery usage of Martin V himself: the Pisan pope Alexander V is "our predecessor," John XXIII "succeeded him in the papacy," and Martin was elected after John's deposition "from the papacy." The Annuario Pontificio now lists both Pisan popes as antipopes, and Angelo Roncalli took the name John XXIII in 1958. See the disputed question on the Pisan popes.

Source and coverage: Martin V names Alexander V "our predecessor" · unreviewed locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
