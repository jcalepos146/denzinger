Innocent XI · 1676–1689

n. 1285 Errors of Michael of Molinos

[Condemned in the decree of the Sacred Office, August 28, and in the Constitutions "Coelestis Pastor," Nov. 20, 1687]

DS 2265Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as heretical, erroneous, scandalous, blasphemous, offensive to pious ears, rash, seditious, respectively

65. One must obey directors in the exterior life, and the latitude of the vow of obedience of religious extends only to the external. In the interior life the matter is different, because only God and the director enter.

Latin text · DS 2265

226565. Praepositis oboediendum est in exteriore, et latitudo voti oboedientiae religiosorum tantummodo ad exterius pertingit. In interiore vero aliter res se habet, ubi (quo) solus Deus et director intrant.
