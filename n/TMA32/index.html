John Paul II · 1978–2005

TMA 32 a) First Phase

[From the Apostolic Letter, "Tertio Millennio Adveniente," Nov. 10, 1994]

Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

... Nevertheless, the joy of every Jubilee is above all a joy based upon the forgiveness of sins, the joy of conversion. It therefore seems appropriate to emphasize once more the theme of the Synod of Bishops in 1984: penance and reconciliation. That Synod was an event of extraordinary significance in the life of the post-conciliar Church. It took up the ever topical question of conversion ( "metanoia" ), which is the pre-condition for reconciliation with God on the part of both individuals and communities.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (RCD 3–TMA 36). The divorced and civilly remarried may not receive communion while the situation lasts, though they remain part of the Church; and the preparation of the Jubilee, including the examination of conscience for the sins of the Church's members.
