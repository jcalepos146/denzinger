Alexander VII · 1655–1667

n. 1127 Various Errors on Moral Matters

[Condemned in decrees of Sept. 24, 1665, and of March 18.1666 A. On the 24th Day of September, 1665

DS 2047Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censures

27. If a book is published by a younger or modern person, its opinion should be considered as probable, since it is not established that it has been rejected by the Holy See as improbable.
