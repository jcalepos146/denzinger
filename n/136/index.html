Council of Ephesus · 431

n. 136 Bishops of the Holy See Concerning the Grace of God

Particular councilEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

Chapter 7. Furthermore that which was determined in the decrees of the synod of Carthage, * we have embraced as the Apostolic See's own, namely, what was defined in the third chapter: "That whoever says that the grace of God, by which we are justified through Jesus Christ our Lord, has power only for the remission of sins which have already been committed, and not also for help, that they may not be committed, let him be anathema." [seen. 103 ].

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (n. 129–142). The Indiculus on grace, compiled about 435–442 (probably by Prosper of Aquitaine) from papal and African statements; later treated as an authoritative papal summary against Semipelagianism.

Source and coverage: Bishops of the Holy See Concerning the Grace of God · unreviewed locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
