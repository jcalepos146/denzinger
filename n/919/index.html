Julius III · 1550–1555 · Session XIV (Nov. 25, 1551)

n. 919 Canons On the Sacrament of Penance

DS 1709Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

Can. 9. If anyone says that the sacramental absolution of the priest is not a judicial act, but an empty service of pronouncing and declaring to the one confessing that his sins are forgiven, provided only that he believes that he has been absolved, or * even if the priest does not absolve seriously, but in jest; or says that the confession of the penitent is not required, so that the priest may be able to absolve him: let him be anathema [cf.n 902 ].

Latin text · DS 1709

1709Can. 9. Si quis dixerit, absolutionem sacramentalem sacerdotis non esse actum iudicialem, sed nudum ministerium pronuntiandi et declarandi, remissa esse peccata confitenti, modo tantum credat se esse absolutum aut sacerdos non serio, sed ioco absolvat; aut dixerit non requiri confessionem paenitentis, ut sacerdos ipsum absolvere possit : an. s. (cf. DS 1685 1462).
