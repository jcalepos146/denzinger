Council of Illiberi · between 300/306

n. 52a The Indissolubility of Matrimony

DS 117Particular councilEditorial grade · Discipline

Can. 9. Likewise let the faithful woman, who has left an adulterous husband and attracts another faithful one, be forbidden to marry; if she should marry, let her not receive communion unless he whom she has left has previously departed this world; unless by chance the exigency of illness should compel the giving.

Latin text · DS 117

117Can.9. Item femina fidelis, quae adulterum maritum reliquerit fidelem et alterum ducit, prohibeatur ne ducat; si duxerit, non prius accipiat communionem, nisi quem reliquerit prius de saeculo exierit; nisi forte necessitas infirmitatis dare compulerit.
