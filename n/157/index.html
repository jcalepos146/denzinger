Council of Chalcedon · 451

n. 157 The Ordination of the Clergy

[From "Ancient Statutes of the Church," or "Ancient Statutes of the East"]

cf. DS 333Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Discipline

Can. 9 (97). When a porter is ordained, after he has been instructed by the archdeacon as to how he ought to live in the house of God, at the suggestion of the archdeacon let the bishop hand him the keys of the church from the altar, saying: So act as if You were about to give God the reason for these things which are opened with those keys.

Latin text · DS 333

333qui dicit quod praescientia Dei hominem violenter compellat (impellat) ad mortem, vel quod Dei pereant voluntate qui pereunt;
