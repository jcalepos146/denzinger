Innocent XI · 1676–1689

n. 1178 Various Errors on Moral Subjects (II)

[Condemned in a decree of the Holy Office, March 4, 1679]

DS 2128Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as scandalous, pernicious, injurious

28. He who has been promoted to a magistracy or a public office by means of a recommendation or a gift can utter with mental reservation the oath which is customarily exacted of similar persons by order of the king, without regard for the intent of the one exacting it, because he is not bound to confess a concealed crime.

Latin text · DS 2128

212828. Qui mediante commendatione vel munere ad magistratum vel officium publicum promotus est, poterit cum restrictione mentali praestare iuramentum, quod de mandato regis a similibus solet exigi, non habito respectu ad intentionem exigentis; quia non tenetur fateri crimen occultum.
