Council of Basel and its aftermath · 1431–1463 · 1446

Supplement B13 Eugene IV's instructions to his legates: Constance and Basel received, saving the Apostolic See

[Raynald, Annales ecclesiastici XXVIII (Bar-le-Duc, 1874), p. 461. Translation and citation contributed to this site; not yet checked against the scan.]

Not in DenzingerEditorial grade · Juridical act

We, inclined by the supplications of the king and the electors, in imitation of the holy Fathers our predecessors (by adhering to whose footsteps we do not doubt that we offer an acceptable service to the divine majesty), just as they were accustomed to receive, embrace and venerate the general councils of their times duly instituted and canonically celebrated, so do we receive, embrace and venerate with all reverence and devotion the general councils of Constance and of Basel, from its beginning up to the translation made by us, without prejudice, however, to the right, dignity and preeminence of the Holy Apostolic See, nor to the power granted by Christ in the person of Blessed Peter to it and to the one canonically seated upon it.

Editorial assessment · Juridical act. Two limits are written into the reception: Basel only up to its transfer (B7), and nothing against the rights of the Apostolic See and the power given to Peter and his successor.
