John Paul II · 1978–2005

FC 20 An Indissoluble Communion

[From the Apostolic Exhortation, "Familiaris Consortio," Nov. 22, 1981]

Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

... It is a fundamental duty of the Church to reaffirm strongly, as the Synod Fathers did, the doctrine of the indissolubility of marriage. To all those who, in our times, consider it too difficult, or indeed impossible, to be bound to one person for the whole of life, and to those caught up in a culture that rejects the indissolubility of marriage and openly mocks the commitment of spouses to fidelity, it is necessary to reconfirm the good news of the definitive nature of that conjugal love that has in Christ its foundation and strength.

Being rooted in the personal and total self-giving of the couple, and being required by the good of the children, the indissolubility of marriage finds its ultimate truth in the plan that God has manifested in His revelation: ...

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (FC 13–FC 32). Marriage as covenant and sacrament; the family as a communion of persons; the transmission of life restated with Humanae Vitae.
