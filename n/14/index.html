Creeds and Early Formulas · 2nd–6th c.

n. 14 The Creed of Epiphanius

Longer Form (Exposition of Nicene Creed proposed to certain catechumens in the Orient)

CreedEditorial grade · Profession of faith

We believe in one catholic and apostolic Church, and in one baptism of repentance, and in the resurrection of the dead, and the just judgment of souls and bodies, and in the kingdom of heaven, and in life eternal.

But those who say that there was a time when the Son or the Holy Spirit was not, that he was made from nothing or is of another substance or essence, alleging that the Son of God or the Holy Spirit was changed or altered, these the catholic and apostolic Church, your mother and our mother, anathematizes. We also anathematize those who do not confess the resurrection of the dead, and besides all the heresies which are not consistent with this true faith.

Editorial assessment · Profession of faith (n. 1–18). Early forms of the creed and related formulas, collected by the editors as witnesses to the baptismal faith.
