Lateran Synod of 649 · 649

n. 259 The Trinity, the Incarnation, etc.

DS 506Particular councilEditorial grade · Definitive

Can. 6. If anyone does not properly and truly confess according to the holy Fathers, that from two and in two natures substantially united unconfusedly and undividedly there is one and the same Lord and God, Jesus Christ, let him be condemned [see n. 148].

Latin text · DS 506

506Can. 6: Si quis secundum sanctos Patres non confitetur proprie et secundum veritatem, ex duabus et in duabus naturis substantialiter unitis inconfuse et indivise unum eundemque esse Dominum et Deum Jesum Christum, condemnatus sit.

Editorial assessment · Definitive (n. 254–274). The canons of the Lateran Synod of 649 under Martin I against monothelitism. Received by Constantinople III; their doctrine, including Mary's perpetual virginity (n. 256), is held as of faith.
