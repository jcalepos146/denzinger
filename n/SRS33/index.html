John Paul II · 1978–2005

SRS 33 Authentic Human Development

[From the Encyclical Letter, "Sollicitudo Rei Socialis," Dec. 30, 1987]

Papal teachingEditorial grade · Prudential decision

Nor would a type of development which did not respect and promote human rights - personal and social, economic and political, including the rights of nations and of peoples - be really worthy of man. ...

Editorial assessment · Prudential decision (SRS 21–SRS 48). The gap between rich and poor, the two blocs, structures of sin, the option for the poor. Its principles restate the Church's teaching; its analysis of the world of 1987 is a judgment of circumstances.
