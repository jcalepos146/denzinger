Julius III · 1550–1555 · Session XIV (Nov. 25, 1551)

n. 928 Canons on Extreme Unction

DS 1718Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

Can. 3. If anyone says that the rite of extreme unction and its practice, which the holy Roman Church observes, is opposed to the statement of the blessed Apostle James, and that it is therefore to be changed, and can be contemned without sin by Christians: let him be anathema [cf. n. 910].
