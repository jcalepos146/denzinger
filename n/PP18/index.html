Paul VI · 1963–1978

PP 18 Development in Proper Perspective

[From the Encyclical Letter, "Populorum Progressio," March 26, 1967]

Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

... The pursuit of life's necessities is quite legitimate; hence we are duty-bound to do the work which enables us to obtain them: "If anyone is unwilling to work, do not let him eat.'' (l6) But the acquisition of worldly goods can lead men to greed, to the unrelenting desire for more, to the pursuit of greater personal power. Rich and poor alike—be they individuals, families or nations—can fall prey to avarice and soulstifling materialism.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (PP 9–PP 31). Development as the new name of peace's preconditions: integral development of the whole person; the universal destination of goods to which property and trade are subordinate; the critique of unchecked liberal capitalism; revolution excluded save in the case of manifest, long-standing tyranny (PP 31).
