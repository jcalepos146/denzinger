The Second Vatican Council · 1962–1965

SC 90 The Divine Office

[From the Constitution on the Sacred Liturgy, "Sacrosanctum Concilium," Dec. 4, 1963]

Conciliar decreeEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

The divine office, because it is the public prayer of the Church, is a source of piety, and nourishment for personal prayer. ...

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (SC 83–SC 104). The Divine Office as the prayer of Christ and his Body, and the meaning of the liturgical year and the feasts of the saints.
