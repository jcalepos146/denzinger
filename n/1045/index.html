St. Pius V · 1566–1572

n. 1045 Errors of Michael du Bay (BAII)

[Condemned in the Bull "Ex omnibus afflictionibus," Oct. 1, 1567]

DS 1945Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as heretical, erroneous, rash, scandalous, respectively

45. The sacrifice of the Mass is a sacrifice for no other reason than for that general one by which "every work is performed that man may be closely connected with God in holy association." *

Latin text · DS 1945

194545. sacrificium Missae non alia ratione est sacrificium, quam generali illa, qua 'omne opus, quod fit, ut sancta societate Deo homo inhaereat'.

Editorial assessment · Condemned, graded censures (n. 1001–1080). Pius V applies a list of censures respectively to the propositions of Baius. The Comma Pianum affects how the concession in the censure clause is read (n. 1080).
