Council of Trent · 1545–1563 · Session VI (Jan. 13, 1547)

n. 812 Canons On Justification

DS 1552Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

Can. 2. If anyone shall say that divine grace through Christ Jesus is given for this only, that man may more easily be able to live justly and merit eternal life, as if by free will without grace he were able to do both, though with difficulty and hardship: let him be anathema [cf. n. 795, 809].

Latin text · DS 1552

1552Can. 2. Si quis dixerit, ad hoc solum divinam gratiam per Christum Jesum dari, ut facilius homo iuste vivere ac vitam aeternam promereri possit, quasi per liberum arbitrium sine gratia utrumque, sed aegre tamen et difficulter possit: an. s. (cf: DS 1524s).
