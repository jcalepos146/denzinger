Council of Ephesus · 431

n. 120 The Anathemas of the Chapter of Cyril (against Nestorius)

DS 259Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Weight disputed

Can. 8. If anyone ventures to say that the assumed man must be worshipped and glorified along with God the Word, and bears the same title with Him, as the one in the other, for the "(Greek text deleted)" always being added will force (one) to understand this, and does not rather honor Emmanuel with one worship and apply one glory to Him, according as the Word was made flesh, let him be anathema.

Latin text · DS 259

2598. Si quis audet dicere adsumptum hominem coadorari oportere Deo Verbo et conglorificari et coappellari Deum ut alter alteri ("co-" enim semper adjectum hoc intelligi cogit) et non magis una adoratione honorificat Emmanuel et unam ei glorificationem refert secundum quod factum est caro Varbum, an.s.

Editorial assessment · Weight disputed (n. 113–124). Cyril's twelve anathemas were read at Ephesus; whether the council formally adopted them is debated. Constantinople II later endorsed their doctrine.
