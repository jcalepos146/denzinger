John Paul II · 1978–2005

HP 10 Homosexualitatis Problema

[From the Letter of the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith on the pastoral care of homosexual persons, "Homosexualitatis Problema," Oct. 1, 1986]

Doctrine of the FaithEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

It is deplorable that homosexual persons have been and are the object of violent malice in speech or in action. Such treatment deserves condemnation from the Church's pastors wherever it occurs. It reveals a kind of disregard for others which endangers the most fundamental principles of a healthy society. The intrinsic dignity of each person must always be respected in word, in action and in law.

But the proper reaction to crimes committed against homosexual persons should not be to claim that the homosexual condition is not disordered. When such a claim is made and when homosexual activity is consequently condoned, or when civil legislation is introduced to protect behavior to which no one has any conceivable right, neither the Church nor society at large should be surprised when other distorted notions and practices gain ground, and irrational and violent reactions increase.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (DEV 10–DVI 8). The Holy Spirit and the sin against the Spirit (Dominum et Vivificantem); the homosexual inclination as objectively disordered and homosexual acts as sinful, with respect owed to persons (Homosexualitatis Problema); the embryo to be respected as a person from conception, and techniques that separate procreation from the conjugal act excluded, including in vitro fertilization (Donum Vitae).
