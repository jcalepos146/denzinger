Councils of Mileum II and Carthage (XVI) · 416 and 418

n. 108 Original Sin and Grace

DS 230Particular councilEditorial grade · Definitive

Can. 8. it has likewise been decided that whoever wishes that the words themselves of the Lord's prayer, where we say:"Forgive us our debts" [ Matt. 6:12] be said by the saints so as to be spoken humbly, not truthfully, let him be anathema. For who would tolerate one praying and lying, not to men, but to the Lord himself, who says with his lips that he wishes to be forgiven, and in his heart holds that he does not have debts to be forgiven?

Latin text · DS 230

230Can. 8. Item placuit, ut quicumque ipsa verba dominicae orationis, ubi dicimus: 'Dimitte nobis debita nostra' (Mt 6,12), ita volunt a sanctis dici, ut humiliter, non veraciter hoc dicatur, an. s. Quis enim ferat orantem et non hominibus, sed ipsi Domino mentientem, qui labiis sibi dicit dimitti velle, et corde dicit, quae sibi dimittantur, debita non habere?

Editorial assessment · Definitive (n. 101–108). Canons of an African council, confirmed by Pope Zosimus and received by the whole Church against Pelagius. Their teaching on the necessity of grace was later defined at Trent.
