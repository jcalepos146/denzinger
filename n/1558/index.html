Pius VI · 1775–1799

n. 1558 Betrothals and Matrimony

[Memorial Booklet about Betrothals, etc. sec. 8]

DS 2658Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as false

58. The proposition which states that betrothals properly so-called contain a mere civil act which disposes for the celebrating of marriage, and that these same betrothals are altogether subject to the prescription of the civil laws. as if the act disposing for the sacrament is not, under this aspect, subject to the law of the Church,—false, harmful to the right of the Church in respect to the effects flowing even from betrothals by reason of the canonical sanctions, derogatory to the discipline established by the Church.

[Matrimony, sees. 7, 11, 12]

Latin text · DS 2658

265858. Propositio, quae statuit, sponsalia proprie dicta actum mere civilem continere, qui ad matrimonium celebrandum disponit, eademque civilium legum praescripto omnino subiacere: quasi actus disponens ad sacramentum non subiaceat sub hac ratione iuri Ecclesiae : - falsa, iuris Ecclesiae quoad effectus etiam e sponsalibus vi canonicarum sanctionum profluentes laesiva, disciplinae ab Ecclesia constitutae derogans.
