John Paul II · 1978–2005

LE 27 Human Work in the Light of the Cross and the Resurrection of Christ

[From the Encyclical Letter, "Laborem Exercens," Sept. 14, 1981]

Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

... The Christian finds in human work a small part of the Cross of Christ and accepts it in the same spirit of redemption in which Christ accepted his Cross for us. In work, thanks to the light that penetrates us from the Resurrection of Christ, we always find a glimmer of new life, of the new good, as if it were an announcement of "the new heavens and the new earth" in which man and the world participate precisely through the toil that goes with work. ... On the one hand this confirms the indispensability of the Cross in the spirituality of human work; on the other hand the Cross which this toil constitutes reveals a new good springing from work itself, from work understood in depth and in all its aspects and never apart from work. ...

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (LE 6–LE 27). Work in its subjective sense; the priority of labour over capital; the just wage and the family; trade unions as indispensable; the spirituality of work. Principles are teaching; the particular means are left to circumstances.
