Paul VI · 1963–1978

CI Decree regarding cases in which impotence renders marriage null

[From the Decree of the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, "Circa Impotentiam," May 13, 1977]

Doctrine of the FaithEditorial grade · Discipline

The Sacred Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith has always held that those who have undergone a vasectomy and those who find themselves in similar circumstances, such that their impotency is not established with certainty, are not impeded from marriage.

Furthermore, having examined this position and after repeated studies conducted by this Sacred Congregation and the Commission for the revision of the Code of Canon Law, the Cardinals and Bishops, members of this Sacred Congregation, in the plenary meeting held on Wednesday, May 11, 1977, chose to respond to the following proposed doubts:

Whether the impotence that renders a marriage null consists in the antecedent and perpetual incapacity, whether absolute or relative, to complete the conjugal act.

If affirmative, whether the conjugal act necessarily requires the ejaculation of semen produced in the testicles.

Regarding the first: affirmative; regarding the second: negative. ...

Editorial assessment · Discipline. Marriage is not to be impeded for men who have had a vasectomy or similar condition, provided they can perform the conjugal act. A juridical decision.
