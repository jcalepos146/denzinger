Pius IX · 1846–1878

n. 1639 Faith and Reason

[From the Encyclical, "Qui pluribus," Nov. 9, 1846]

DS 2780Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

And so, human reason, knowing clearly and openly from these most splendid and equally strong proofs that God is the author of the same faith, can proceed no further; but, having completely cast aside and removed every difficulty and doubt, it should render all obedience to this faith, since it holds as certain that whatever faith itself proposes to man to be believed or to be done, has been transmitted by God.*

Latin text · DS 2780

2780(obligatio credendi) Itaque humana ratio ex splendidissimis hisce aeque ac firmissimis argumentis clare aperteque cognoscens, Deum eiusdem fidei auctorem exsistere, ulterius progredi nequit, sed quavis difficultate ac dubitatione penitus abiecta atque remota, omne eidem fidei obsequium praebeat oportet, cum pro certo habeat, a Deo traditum esse, quidquid fides ipsa hominibus credendum et agendum proponit.
