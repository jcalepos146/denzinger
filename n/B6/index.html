Council of Basel and its aftermath · 1431–1463 · Sessions 20, 21 and 23 (1435–1436)

Supplement B6 Reform decrees

[Summary written for this site; the texts are not reproduced.]

Not in DenzingerEditorial grade · Discipline

Session 20 (22 January 1435): penalties against clerics living in concubinage; the faithful need not shun excommunicated persons unless they are named or notorious; interdicts are not to be imposed lightly.

Session 21 (9 June 1435): annates and similar payments to the curia abolished; rules for the reverent celebration of the divine office.

Session 23 (March 1436): rules for papal elections and an oath and profession to be made by the pope; the number of cardinals limited to twenty-four, with requirements of age and learning; papal reservations of benefices restricted.

Editorial assessment · Discipline. Reform law. Some of it, such as the rule on shunning excommunicates, matched what Martin V had already granted; much of it entered national settlements such as the Pragmatic Sanction of Bourges (1438), which Lateran V later annulled (n. 740).
