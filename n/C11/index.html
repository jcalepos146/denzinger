Supplement C11 Martin V to the Council of Pavia–Siena: Constance's acts on the schism are not to be questioned

[Martin V's instructions for the council called under Frequens. Mansi 28, col. 1080. Translation and citation contributed to this site; not yet checked against the scan.]

Not in DenzingerEditorial grade · Juridical act

In such a manner, however, that it shall not hereby be understood that permission has been granted to call into question what was wholesomely defined by the sacred Council of Constance regarding the sedition of the past schism, the deposition of those who were then contending for the papacy, and the inspired elevation of this same Lord our Pope to the apostolate, or to propose or attempt anything in any way contrary to these decisions or any part of them.

Editorial assessment · Juridical act. Martin V protects the acts that ended the schism (the depositions and his own election) from review by the next council. It is a papal act about Constance, not a conciliar decree.
