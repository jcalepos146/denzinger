John Paul II · 1978–2005

DVI 3 Anthropology and Procedures in the Biomedical Field

[From the Instruction of the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith on respect for human life in its origin and on the dignity of procreation, "Donum Vitae," Feb. 22, 1987]

Doctrine of the FaithEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

... No, for the same reasons which lead one to reject heterologous artificial fertilization: for it is contrary to the unity of marriage and to the dignity of the procreation of the human person. Surrogate motherhood represents an objective failure to meet the obligations of maternal love, of conjugal fidelity and of responsible motherhood; it offends the dignity and the right of the child to be conceived, carried in the womb, brought into the world and brought up by his own parents; it sets up, to the detriment of families, a division between the physical, psychological and moral elements which constitute those families. ...

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (DEV 10–DVI 8). The Holy Spirit and the sin against the Spirit (Dominum et Vivificantem); the homosexual inclination as objectively disordered and homosexual acts as sinful, with respect owed to persons (Homosexualitatis Problema); the embryo to be respected as a person from conception, and techniques that separate procreation from the conjugal act excluded, including in vitro fertilization (Donum Vitae).

Source and coverage: Donum Vitae · excerpts locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
