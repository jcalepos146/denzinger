Lateran Council IV · 1215

n. 439 Chap. 41.The Continuation of Good Faith in Every Precept

DS 816Conciliar definitionEditorial grade · Discipline

Since "everything... which is not from faith is a sin" [ Rom. 14:23 ], by synodal judgment we define that no precept either canonical or civil without good faith has any value, since that which cannot be observed without mortal sin must in general be rejected by every constitution and custom. Therefore, it is necessary that he who lay down a rule at no time be conscious of anything wrong.

Latin text · DS 816

816Quoniam 'omne quod non est ex fide, peccatum est' (Rom 14, 23), synodali iudicio diffinimus, ut nulla valeat absque bona fide praescriptio tam canonica quam civilis, cum generaliter sit omni constitutione atque consuetudini derogandum, quae absque mortali peccato non potest observari. Unde oportet, ut, qui praescribit, in nulla temporis parte rei habeat conscientiam alienae. 1.273
