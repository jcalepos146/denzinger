Leo XIII · 1878–1903

n. 1882 The Christian Constitution of States

[From the Encyclical "Immortale Dei," November 1, 1885]

cf. DS 3184Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

It is also in the public interest to give attention wisely to the affairs of municipal administration, and in this to strive especially to effect that consideration be given publicly to the formation of youth in religion and in good conduct, in that manner which is right for Christians. On these things especially does the safety of the individual states depend.
