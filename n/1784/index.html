The Vatican Council · 1869–1870 · Session III (April 24, 1870)

n. 1784 Chap. 1. God, Creator of All Things

DS 3003Conciliar decreeEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

[The result of creation].But God protects and governs by His providence all things which He created, "reaching from end to end mightily and ordering all things sweetly" [cf. Wisd. 8:1]. For "all things are naked and open to His eyes" [ Heb. 4:13], even those which by the free action of creatures are in the future.

Latin text · DS 3003

3003Universa vero, quae condidit, Deus providentia sua tuetur atque gubernat, 'attingens a fine usque ad finem fortiter et disponens omnia suaviter' (cf. Sap 8, 1).'Omnia enim nuda et aperta sunt oculis eius' (Hebr 4, 13), ea etiam, quae libera creaturarum actione futura sunt. 1.626
