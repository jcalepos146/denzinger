Council of Basel and its aftermath · 1431–1463 · 1447–1449

Supplement B12 The end of Basel

[Summary written for this site.]

Not in DenzingerEditorial grade · Juridical act

Concordats with the German princes (1447–1448) settled the German Church's relation to Rome. The assembly moved to Lausanne in 1448. Felix V abdicated, and on 25 April 1449 the assembly recognized Nicholas V and declared itself dissolved.

In 1460 Pius II forbade appeals from the pope to a future council (n. 717), and Lateran V (1516) taught that the pope alone has authority to convoke, transfer and dissolve councils (n. 740).

Editorial assessment · Juridical act. The settlement that closed the conciliar crisis.
