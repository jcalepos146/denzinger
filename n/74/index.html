Council of Rome · 382

n. 74 The Trinity and the Incarnation

[Tome of DAMASUS *]

DS 168Particular councilEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

(16) If anyone does not say that the Holy Spirit, just as the Son, is truly and properly of the Father, of divine substance, and is true God, he is a heretic.

Latin text · DS 168

168(16.) Si quis non dixerit, Spiritum Sanctum de Patre esse vere ac proprie, sicut Filium, de divina substantia et Deum verum: haereticus est.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (n. 58–82). The anathemas of Pope Damasus's Roman synod (382); widely received as a statement of faith, though not an ecumenical definition.
