Pius IV · 1559–1565 · Session XXI (July 16, 1562)

n. 932 Received under Either Species

DS 1729Conciliar decreeEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

Moreover, it declares that although our Redeemer, as has been said before, at that Last Supper instituted this sacrament and gave it to the apostles under two species, yet it must be confessed that Christ whole and entire and a true sacrament is received even under either species alone, and that on that account, as far as regards its fruit, those who receive only one species are not to be deprived of any grace which is necessary for salvation [can. 3].

Chap. 4. Little Children are not

Latin text · DS 1729

1729Insuper declarat, quamvis Redemptor noster, ut antea dictum est, in suprema illa Coena hoc sacramentum in duabus speciebus instituerit et Apostolis tradiderit: tamen fatendum esse, etiam sub altera tantum specie totum atque integrum Christum verumque sacramentum sumi, ac propterea, quod ad fructum attinet, nulla gratia necessaria ad salutem eos defraudari, qui unam speciem solam accipiunt (can. 3).
