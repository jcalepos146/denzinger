Lateran Council V · 1512–1517

n. 753 Errors of Martin Luther

[Condemned in the Bull "Exsurge Domine," June 15, 1520]

DS 1463Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as heretical, scandalous, false, offensive to pious ears, seductive of simple minds, respectively

13. In the sacrament of penance and the remission of sin the pope or the bishop does no more than the lowest priest; indeed, where there is no priest, any Christian, even if a woman or child, may equally do as much.
