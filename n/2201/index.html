Pius XI · 1922–1939

n. 2201 Masturbation Procured Directly

[From the Decree of the Holy Office, August 2, 1929]

DS 3684Curial responseEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

Whether masturbation procured directly is permitted to obtain sperm, by which a contagious diseasebIenorragia(gonorrhea) may be detected and, insofar as it can be done, cured.

Reply: In the negative.

Iul. ): Negative. 1.782 2202 Quoniam omnis educandi ratio ad eam spectat hominis conformationem, quam is in hac mortali vita adipiscatur oportet, ut destinatum sibi a Creatore finem supremum contingat, liquido patet, ut nulla veri nominis educatio esse potest, quae ad finem ultimum non ordinetur tota, ita, praesenti hoc rerum ordine Dei providentia constituto, postquam scilicet se ipse in Unigenito suo revelavit, qui unus 'via, veritas et vita' (Io 14, 6) est, plenam perfectamque educationem dari non posse, nisi eam, quae christiana vocatur. ...
