John Paul II · 1978–2005

HP 16 Homosexualitatis Problema

[From the Letter of the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith on the pastoral care of homosexual persons, "Homosexualitatis Problema," Oct. 1, 1986]

Doctrine of the FaithEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

... The human person, made in the image and likeness of God, can hardly be adequately described by a reductionist reference to his or her sexual orientation. Everyone living on the face of the earth has personal problems and difficulties, but challenges to growth, strengths, talents and gifts as well. Today, the Church provides a badly needed context for the care of the human person when she refuses to consider the person solely as a "heterosexual" or a "homosexual" and insists that every person has a fundamental identity: the creature of God, and by grace, his child and heir to eternal life.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (DEV 10–DVI 8). The Holy Spirit and the sin against the Spirit (Dominum et Vivificantem); the homosexual inclination as objectively disordered and homosexual acts as sinful, with respect owed to persons (Homosexualitatis Problema); the embryo to be respected as a person from conception, and techniques that separate procreation from the conjugal act excluded, including in vitro fertilization (Donum Vitae).
