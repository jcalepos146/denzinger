Council of Basel and its aftermath · 1431–1463 · 28 March 1447

Supplement B16 Nicholas V confirms Eugene IV's concessions to the German nation

[Mercati, Raccolta di Concordati I (1919), pp. 176–177 ("Niccolò V approva e conferma le concessioni fatte da Eugenio IV alla Nazione Germanica"). Located from the volume's table of contents; the text has not been checked for this site.]

Not in DenzingerEditorial grade · Juridical act

Three weeks after his election, Nicholas V approved and confirmed the concessions Eugene IV had made to the German nation in February 1447 (B14).

Editorial assessment · Juridical act. Whether it names Ad ea ex debito, or the reservation (B15), has still to be read in Mercati. Nicholas V then concluded the Concordat of Vienna (1448), confirmed by the bull Ad sacram Petri sedem of 19 March 1448.
