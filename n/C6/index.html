Council of Constance: decrees not in Denzinger · 1414–1418 · Session 43 (March 1418)

Supplement C6 Reform decrees under Martin V

[Summary written for this site; the texts are not reproduced.]

Not in DenzingerEditorial grade · Discipline

Martin V and the council revoked exemptions granted since the schism, and unions and incorporations of benefices made without cause.

They reserved the income of vacant benefices to their proper uses, renewed the penalties against simony, limited dispensations, restricted papal taxes on the clergy, and set rules for the life, dress and conduct of clerics. Other reforms were left to concordats with the nations.

Editorial assessment · Discipline. Disciplinary reform, approved by Martin V.
