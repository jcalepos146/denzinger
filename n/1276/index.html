Innocent XI · 1676–1689

n. 1276 Errors of Michael of Molinos

[Condemned in the decree of the Sacred Office, August 28, and in the Constitutions "Coelestis Pastor," Nov. 20, 1687]

DS 2256Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as heretical, erroneous, scandalous, blasphemous, offensive to pious ears, rash, seditious, respectively

56. Two laws and two desires (the one of the soul, the other of self-love) endure as long as self-love endures; wherefore, when this is purged and dead, as happens through the interior way, those two laws and two desires are no longer present; nor, is any lapse incurred further, nor, is anything felt more, not even venial sin.

Latin text · DS 2256

225656. Duae leges et duae cupiditates (animae una, et amoris proprii altera) tamdiu perdurant, quamdiu perdurat amor proprius: unde quando hic purgatus est et mortuus, uti fit per viam internam, non adsunt amplius illae duae leges et duae cupiditates, nec ulterius lapsus aliquis incurritur, nec aliquid sentitur amplius, ne quidem veniale peccatum.
