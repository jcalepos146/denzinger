TC Traditionis Custodes

[From the Apostolic Letter (Motu Proprio), "Traditionis Custodes," July 16, 2021]

Papal teachingEditorial grade · Discipline

Art. 1. The liturgical books promulgated by Saint Paul VI and Saint John Paul II, in conformity with the decrees of Vatican Council II, are the unique expression of the lex orandi of the Roman Rite.

Art. 2. It belongs to the diocesan bishop, as moderator, promoter, and guardian of the whole liturgical life of the particular Church entrusted to him, to regulate the liturgical celebrations of his diocese. Therefore, it is his exclusive competence to authorize the use of the 1962 Roman Missal in his diocese, according to the guidelines of the Apostolic See.

Art. 8. Previous norms, instructions, permissions, and customs that do not conform to the provisions of the present Motu Proprio are abrogated.

Editorial assessment · Discipline. The liturgical books of Paul VI and John Paul II are the unique expression of the lex orandi of the Roman rite; the use of the 1962 Missal depends on the diocesan bishop; earlier norms contrary to this are abrogated.

Editorial relationship: TC changes-discipline SUMP

Traditionis Custodes changes the framework for use of the 1962 Missal and abrogates incompatible prior norms. This relationship concerns discipline; it does not identify a reversal of dogma or claim every earlier norm was abrogated.

Previous norms, instructions, permissions, and customs that do not conform to the provisions of the present Motu Proprio are abrogated.

TC

For such a celebration with either Missal, the priest needs no permission from the Apostolic See or from his own Ordinary.
