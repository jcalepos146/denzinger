Innocent XII · 1691–1700

n. 5006 Matrimony as a Contract and a Sacrament

[Reply of the Holy Office to Mission. Capucc., July 23, 1698]

DS 2340Curial responseEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

Whether matrimony between apostates from the faith and those previously rightly baptized, entered upon after the apostasy, publicly according to the custom of pagans or Mohammedans, is truly matrimony and a sacrament.

Reply: If any agreement of dissolubility be at hand, there is no matrimony and no sacrament, but if none is at hand, there is matrimony and a sacrament

Latin text · DS 2340

2340Qu.: An matrimonium inter apostatas a fide, et antea rite baptizatos, post apostasiam, publice more gentilium vel Mahumetanorum initum, sit vere matrimonium et sacramentum. Resp.: Si adsit pactum dissolubilitatis, non esse matrimonium neque sacramentum; si vero non adsit, esse matrimonium et sacramentum. 1.492 1.493
