Council of Basel and its aftermath · 1431–1463 · 5 February 1447

Supplement B15 Eugene IV's protest: no prejudice to the Apostolic See

[Mercati, Raccolta di Concordati I (1919), p. 176 ("Protesta di Eugenio IV"); often called the Salvatoria. Summary written for this site; the text has not been checked.]

Not in DenzingerEditorial grade · Juridical act

On the same day as the concessions, the dying pope declared that, unable to weigh everything in his illness and acting for the peace of the German Church, he did not intend by them to depart from the teaching of the Fathers or to prejudice the rights and authority of the Apostolic See, and that anything in them to that effect was to be held as not granted.

Editorial assessment · Juridical act. This is why historians call the 1447 settlement clouded: the concessions (B14) and the reservation were issued together. Summary only; check the text in Mercati before citing.

Source and coverage: Protesta di Eugenio IV · summary locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
