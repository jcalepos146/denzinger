Innocent XI · 1676–1689

n. 1150 Frequent and Daily Communion

[From the Decree C. S. Conc., Feb. 12. 1679]

DS 2095Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

Furthermore, let bishops and priests or confessors refute those who hold that daily communion is of divine right,... Let them not permit that a confession of venial sins be made to a simple priest without the approbation of a bishop or ordinary.
