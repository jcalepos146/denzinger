Council of Trent · 1545–1563 · Session VII (March 3, 1547)

n. 860 Canons on the Sacrament of Baptism

DS 1617Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

Can. 4. If anyone shall say that the baptism, which is also given by heretics in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, with the intention of doing what the Church does, is not true baptism: let him be anathema.
