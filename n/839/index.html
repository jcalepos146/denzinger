Council of Trent · 1545–1563 · Session VI (Jan. 13, 1547)

n. 839 Canons On Justification

DS 1579Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

Can. 29. If anyone shall say that he who has fallen after baptism cannot by the grace of God rise again; or that he can indeed recover lost justice, but by faith alone without the sacrament of penance, contrary to what the holy Roman and universal Church, taught by Christ the Lord and His apostles, has hitherto professed, observed, and taught: let him be anathema [cf. n. 807].

Latin text · DS 1579

1579Can. 29.
