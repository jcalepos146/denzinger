Lateran Council IV · 1215

n. 433 Chap.2.The Error of Abbot Joachim

DS 807–808Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as impious, heretical, insane

Yet on this account we do not wish to detract from the monastery in Florence (whose founder is Joachim himself), since both the institution there is regular and the observance salutary, especially since Joachim himself has ordered all his writings to be assigned to us, to be approved or even corrected by the judgment of the Apostolic See, dictating a letter which he signed with his own hand in which he firmly confesses that he holds that Faith which the Roman Church, which (the Lord disposing) is the mother and master of all the faithful, holds. We reprove also and we condemn that very perverse dogma of the impious Almaricus, whose mind the father of lies has so blinded that his doctrine must be considered not so heretical as insane.

Latin text · DS 807–808

807In nullo tamen propter hoc Florensi monasterio (cuius ipse Ioachim exstitit institutor) volumus derogari: quoniam ibi et regularis est institutio, et observantia salutaris: maxime, cum ipse Ioachim omnia scripta sua nobis assignari mandaverit, Apostolicae Sedis iudicio approbanda seu etiam corrigenda, dictans epistolam, quam propria manu subscripsit, in qua firmiter confitetur, se illam fidem tenere, quam Romana tenet Ecclesia, quae (disponente Domino) cunctorum fidelium mater est et magistra.

808Reprobamus etiam et condemnamus perversissimum dogma impii Almarici, cuius mentem sic pater mendacii excaecavit, ut eius doctrina non tam haeretica censenda sit, quam insana. 1.270

Editorial assessment · Condemned, graded censures (n. 431–433). Lateran IV's condemnation of Abbot Joachim's account of the divine unity, with an explicit refusal to condemn his monastery or person.
