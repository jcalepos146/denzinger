PD 10 Christ, Savior and Salvation

[From the Letter of the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith on certain aspects of Christian salvation, "Placuit Deo," Feb. 22, 2018]

Doctrine of the FaithEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

Moreover it is clear that the salvation that Jesus brought in his own person does not occur only in an interior manner. In fact, the Son was made flesh, in order to communicate to every person the salvific communion with God (cf. Jn 1:14). It was precisely by assuming flesh (cf. Rom 8:3; Heb 2:14; 1 Jn 4:2), and being born of a woman (cf. Gal 4:4), that “the Son of God was made the son of man” and our brother (cf. Heb 2:14). Thus, inasmuch as He became part of the human family, “He has united Himself in some fashion with every man and woman” and has established a new order of relationships with God, his Father, and with all humanity in which we can be incorporated in order to participate in the Son of God’s own life. As a result, rather than limiting the salvific action, assuming flesh allows Christ in a concrete way to mediate the salvation of God for all of the sons and daughters of Adam.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (PD 2–PD 15). Salvation in Christ against neo-Pelagian individualism and neo-Gnostic disregard of the body; the Church as the place where salvation is received. Approved by Francis.
