Council of Trent · 1545–1563 · Session VI (Jan. 13, 1547)

n. 821 Canons On Justification

DS 1561Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

Can. 11. If anyone shall say that men are justified either by the sole imputation of the justice of Christ, or by the sole remission of sins, to the exclusion of grace and charity, which is poured forth in their hearts by the Holy Spirit and remains in them, or even that the grace by which we are justified is only the favor of God: let him be anathema [cf. n. 799ff., 809].

Latin text · DS 1561

1561Can. 11.
