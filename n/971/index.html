Pius IV · 1559–1565 · Session XXIV (Nov. 11, 1563)

n. 971 Doctrine (Concerning the Sacrament of Matrimony)

DS 1801Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

Can. 1. If anyone says that matrimony is not truly and properly one of the seven sacraments of the evangelical Law, instituted by Christ the Lord,. but that it has been invented by men in the Church, and does not confer grace: let him be anathema [cf. n. 969 f.].

Latin text · DS 1801

1801Can. 1. Si quis dixerit, matrimonium non esse vere et proprie unum ex septem Legis evangelicae sacramentis, a Christo Domino institutum, sed ab hominibus in Ecclesia inventum, neque gratiam conferre: anathema sit (cf. DS 1800).
