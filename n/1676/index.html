Pius IX · 1846–1878

n. 1676 The False Freedom of Science (against James Frohschammer)

[From the epistle, "Gravissimas inter,', to the Archbishop of Munich-Freising, Dec. 11, 1862]

DS 2861Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

Therefore, the Church, by the power entrusted to it by its divine Founder, has not only the right, but particularly the duty of not tolerating but of proscribing and condemning all errors, if the integrity of faith and the salvation of souls so demand; and on every philosopher who wishes to be a son of the Church, and also on philosophy, it lays this duty—never to say anything against those things which the Church teaches, and to retract those about which the Church has warned them Moreover, We proclaim and declare that a doctrine which teaches the contrary is entirely erroneous and especially harmful to faith itself, to the Church and its authority.
