John Paul II · 1978–2005

RM 15 Characteristics of the Kingdom and Its Demands

[From the Encyclical Letter, "Redemptoris Missio," Dec. 7, 1990]

Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

... The kingdom is the concern of everyone: individuals, society, and the world. Working for the kingdom means acknowledging and promoting God's activity, which is present in human history and transforms it. Building the kingdom means working for liberation from evil in all its forms. In a word, the kingdom of God is the manifestation and the realization of God's plan of salvation in all its fullness.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (RM 5–RM 92). Christ the one Saviour and the universal salvific will; the Kingdom not separable from Christ or the Church; the permanent validity of the mission ad gentes; dialogue as part of mission.
