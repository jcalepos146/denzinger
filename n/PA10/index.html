John Paul II · 1978–2005

PA 10 The Teaching of the Magisterium

[From the Instruction of the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith on Infant Baptism, "Pastoralis Actio," Oct. 20, 1980]

Doctrine of the FaithEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

In view of this, Christ's warning in St. John's Gospel, "unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God," must be taken as an invitation of universal and limitless love, the words of a Father calling all His children and wishing them to have the greatest of blessings. This pressing and irrevocable call cannot leave us indifferent or neutral, since its acceptance is a condition for achieving our destiny. ...

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (CT 27–DM 12). Catechesis, the Eucharist, euthanasia (the Congregation's declaration Iura et Bona: no one may directly kill an innocent person; proportionate means), infant baptism, and divine mercy.
