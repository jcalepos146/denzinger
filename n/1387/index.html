Clement XI · 1700–1721

n. 1387 Errors of Paschasius Quesnel

[Condemned in the dogmatic Constitution, "Unigenitus," * Sept. 8, 1713]

DS 2437Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as false, captious, evil-sounding, offensive to pious ears, scandalous, pernicious, rash, injurious, seditious, impious, blasphemous, suspected of heresy, erroneous, heretical, respectively

37. The grace of Adam by sanctifying him in himself was proportionate to him; Christian grace, by sanctifying us in Jesus Christ, is omnipotent, and worthy of the Son of God.

Latin text · DS 2437

243737. Gratia Adami, sanctificando illum in semetipso, erat illi proportionata: gratia christiana, nos sanctificando in Jesu Christo, est omnipotens et digna Filio Dei. - Eph 1, 6.

Editorial assessment · Condemned, graded censures (n. 1351–1451). Clement XI's Unigenitus applies a list of censures respectively to all 101 propositions. The bull gives no individual grades.

Source and coverage: Unigenitus · unreviewed locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
