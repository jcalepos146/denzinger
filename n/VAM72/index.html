VAM 72 Chapter IV The Centrality of the Kerygma

[From the Note of the Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith on the transmission of the faith in an epochal change, "Venite ad Me," Oct. 22, 2026]

Doctrine of the FaithEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

Faced with the situation described above, it seems entirely natural that believers should embark on a renewed period of proclaiming the Gospel, finally responding to what Pope Francis called for in Evangelii Gaudium regarding the urgent need for the entire Church to launch a new phase of evangelization. This call was reiterated by Pope Leo XIV, who recognizes the need for a “Christ-centered and kerygmatic” mission, one that is “born of an encounter with Christ that is capable of transforming lives and spreading through attraction rather than conquest. It is an integral mission, holding in balance explicit proclamation, witness, commitment and dialogue, and yielding neither to the temptation of proselytism nor to a merely institutional mentality of preservation or expansion.” We do this not primarily to respond to a new epochal challenge, but out of fidelity to Christian identity itself, which necessarily includes the gift of self in transmitting Christ’s light. With all this in view, it is appropriate to reflect on the theme of proclaiming the kerygma and how it reorients the life and mission of the Church.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (VAM 2–VAM 74). The transmission of the faith in an epochal change: the centrality of the kerygma and of the encounter with Christ. Approved by Leo XIV.
