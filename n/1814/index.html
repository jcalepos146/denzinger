The Vatican Council · 1869–1870 · Session III (April 24, 1870)

n. 1814 Chap. 4. Faith and reason

DS 3035Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

5. [The liberty of faith and the necessity of grace: against Hermes (see n.1618 ff.) ]. If anyone shall have said that the assent of the Christian faith is not free, but is necessarily produced by proofs from human reasoning; or, that the grace of God is necessary only for that living faith "which worketh by charity" [ Gal. 5:6]: let him be anathema [cf. n 1791]

Latin text · DS 3035

30355. Si quis dixerit, assensum fidei christianae non esse liberum sed argumentis humanae rationis necessario produci; aut ad solam fidem vivam, quae per caritatem operatur (Gal 5, 6), gratiam Dei necessariam esse: an. s. (cf. DS 3010).
