Urban V · 1362–1370

n. 575 Errors of Dionysius Foullechat (Perfection and Poverty)

[Condemned in the order "Ex suprernae clementiae dono," Dec. 23, 1368]

DS 1087Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as false, erroneous, heretical

(1) This blessed, indeed most blessed and sweetest law, namely, the law of love, takes away all propriety and power,—false, erroneous, heretical.

Latin text · DS 1087

1087(Art. 4, conclusio 3) Quod haec benedicta, immo superbenedicta lex et dulcissima, videlicet lex amoris ... omnem aufert proprietatem et dominium ...: - revoco tamquam falsam, erroneam et haereticam, quia Christus et Apostoli illam legem perfectissime tenuerunt, et multi alii statuum diversorum legem hanc ... tenuerunt ..., qui proprietatem et dominium habuerunt ...

Not yet proofread: as the source page gives it.

Editorial assessment · Condemned, graded censures (n. 575–580). Urban V and Gregory XI condemn errors on poverty and on the Eucharist.

Source and coverage: Ex suprernae clementiae dono · unreviewed locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
