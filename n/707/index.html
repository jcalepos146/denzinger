Council of Florence · 1438–1445

n. 707 A Decree in Behalf of the Jacobites

[From the Bull "Cantata Domino," February 4, Florentine style, 1441, modern, 1442]

Conciliar canonEditorial grade · Defined dogma

Besides it anathematizes the madness of the Manichaeans, who have established two first principles, one of the visible, and another of the invisible; and they have said that there is one God of the New Testament, another God of the Old Testament.

Editorial assessment · Defined dogma (n. 703–714). Florence's decree for the Jacobites: the Trinity, the canon, and the necessity of the Church. Its statement on salvation is read with Pius IX on invincible ignorance (n. 1677).

Source and coverage: Cantate Domino: decree of union with the Copts and Ethiopians · excerpts locally. Passage grades are editorial assessments.
