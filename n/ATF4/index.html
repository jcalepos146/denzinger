John Paul II · 1978–2005

ATF 4 John Paul Ii

[From the Apostolic Letter (Motu Proprio), "Ad Tuendam Fidem," June 30, 1998]

Papal teachingEditorial grade · Juridical act

... § 2. Furthermore, each and everything set forth definitively by the Magisterium of the Church regarding teaching on faith and morals must be firmly accepted and held; namely, those things required for the holy keeping and faithful exposition of the deposit of faith; therefore, anyone who rejects propositions which are to be held definitively sets himself against the teaching of the Catholic Church. ...

Editorial assessment · Juridical act. Canon law is amended to require assent to truths held definitively and to provide a penalty for those who reject them. A juridical act.
