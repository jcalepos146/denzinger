John XXIII · 1958–1963

PT 137 Connection Between the Common Good and Political Authority

[From the Encyclical Letter, "Pacem in Terris," April 11, 1963]

DS 3993Papal teachingEditorial grade · Prudential decision

Today the universal common good presents us with problems which are world-wide in their dimensions; problems, therefore, which cannot be solved except by a public authority with power, organization and means co-extensive with these problems, and with a world-wide sphere of activity. Consequently the moral order itself demands the establishment of some such general form of public authority.

Editorial assessment · Prudential decision (PT 130–PT 146). That the universal common good now calls for a public authority of world-wide competence, set up by common accord, and under subsidiarity; and an exhortation to Catholics to take part in public life. The principle is the common good; the institution proposed is a prudential judgment.
