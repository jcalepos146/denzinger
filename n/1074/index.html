St. Pius V · 1566–1572

n. 1074 Errors of Michael du Bay (BAII)

[Condemned in the Bull "Ex omnibus afflictionibus," Oct. 1, 1567]

DS 1974Condemned propositionEditorial grade · Condemned, graded censuresCensured as heretical, erroneous, rash, scandalous, respectively

74. Concupiscence in the regenerated who have fallen back into mortal sin, and in those in whom it dominates, is a sin, as also are other bad habits.

Latin text · DS 1974

197474. (71). Concupiscentia in renatis relapsis in peccatum mortale, in quibus iam dominatur, peccatum est, sicut et alii habitus pravi.
