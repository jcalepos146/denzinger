Pius IX · 1846–1878

n. 1650 False Traditionalism (against Augustine Bonnetty)

[From the Decree of the S.C. of the Index, 11, (15) June, 1855]

DS 2812Papal teachingEditorial grade · Profession of faith

2. Reason can prove with certitude the existence of God, the spirituality of the soul, the freedom of man. Faith is posterior to revelation, and hence it cannot be conveniently alleged to prove the existence of God to an atheist, or to prove the spirituality and the freedom of the rational soul against a follower of naturalism and fatalism [cf. n.1622,1625 ].

Latin text · DS 2812

28122. Ratiocinatio Dei exsistentiam, animae spiritualitatem, hominis libertatem cum certitudine probare potest. Fides posterior est revelatione, proindeque ad probandam Dei exsistentiam contra atheum, ad probandam animae rationalis spiritualitatem ac libertatem contra naturalismi ac fatalismi sectatorem allegari convenienter nequit (cf. DS 2751 2754).
