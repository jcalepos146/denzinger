The Vatican Council · 1869–1870 · Session IV (July 18, 1870)

n. 1838 Chap. 4. The Infallible "Magisterium" of the Roman Pontiff

DS 3072Conciliar decreeEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

[Definition of infallibility]. But since in this very age, in which the salutary efficacy of the apostolic duty is especially required, not a few are found who disparage its authority, We deem it most necessary to assert solemnly the prerogative which the Only-begotten Son of God deigned to enjoin with the highest pastoral office.

Latin text · DS 3072

3072At vero cum hac ipsa aetate, qua salutifera Apostolici muneris efficacia vel maxime requiritur, non pauci inveniantur, qui illius auctoritati obtrectant, necessarium omnino esse censemus, praerogativam, quam unigenitus Dei Filius cum summo pastorali officio coniungere dignatus est, solemniter asserere.
