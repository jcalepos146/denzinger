John Paul II · 1978–2005

CA 28 Towards the "new Things" of Today

[From the Encyclical Letter, "Centesimus Annus," May 1, 1991]

Papal teachingEditorial grade · Authoritative teaching

... These resources could become even more abundant if, in place of war, reliable procedures for the resolution of conflicts could be set up, with the resulting spread of the principle of arms control and arms reduction, also in the countries of the Third World, through the adoption of appropriate measures against the arms trade. But it will be necessary above all to abandon a mentality in which the poor — as individuals and as peoples — are considered a burden, as irksome intruders trying to consume what others have produced. ... The advancement of the poor constitutes a great opportunity for the moral, cultural and even economic growth of all humanity.

Editorial assessment · Authoritative teaching (CA 5–CA 29). Rerum Novarum re-read: the dignity of work, private property and its social mortgage, the State's role; the causes of 1989.
