John Paul II · 1978–2005

CCC 90 Catechism of the Catholic Church

[From the Catechism of the Catholic Church, Oct. 11, 1992]

Catechism

The mutual connections between dogmas, and their coherence, can be found in the whole of the Revelation of the mystery of Christ. "In Catholic doctrine there exists an order or hierarchy 234 of truths, since they vary in their relation to the foundation of the Christian faith."
