Vigilius · (537) 540–555

n. 205 Canons against Origen

[From the Book against Origen of the Emperor Justinian, 543]

DS 405Papal teachingEditorial grade · Definitive

Can. 3. If anyone says or holds that the body of our Lord Jesus Christ was first formed in the womb of the holy Virgin, and that after this God, the Word, and the soul, since it had pre-existed, were united to it, let him be anathema.

Latin text · DS 405

405Can.3. Si quis dicit aut sentit, primum formatum esse corpus Domini nostri Jesu Christi in utero beatae Virginis, ac postea unitum ei esse Deum Verbum, et animam, utpote quae ante fuisset, an. s.

Not yet proofread: as the source page gives it.

Editorial assessment · Definitive (n. 203–211). Anathemas against Origenism from the synod of Constantinople (543), accepted by Pope Vigilius; whether Constantinople II adopted them is debated. Their teaching that the punishment of the damned has no end is of faith.
