# Post–Pius XII: candidate documents

Working list for choosing what to import. Not published by the build.

**1958–2001** comes from Neuner–Dupuis, *The Christian Faith*, 7th ed. (2001). Not yet extracted: the PDF can't be downloaded from this session yet.

**2001 onward** is listed below. Marks:
- ✓ = checked against the vatican.va indexes on 2026-10-29: the DDF's complete list of documents, and the Leo XIV indexes.
- No mark = from memory; check the date and title against vatican.va before importing.

Bold = a strong candidate (defines, declares or authoritatively settles a doctrinal point, or is the main source on it).

## Open questions

- **IDs:** new entries carry no older number. Proposal: `H` plus the DH number where DH gives one (`H4102`). Documents after the latest DH edition would get the year and a running number (`H2025-1`).
- **Weights:** the existing grades fit well. A Vatican II dogmatic constitution is `authentic` unless it restates a definition. A DDF responsum is `authentic`. A curial disciplinary norm is `discipline`.
- **Passages:** Denzinger excerpts documents rather than giving them whole, and the new entries should do the same: one entry per doctrinally weighty paragraph, not whole encyclicals.
